Overview

Intelsat is a global satellite operator; in July 2025 it became part of SES. I worked on the Intelsat website, building custom Gutenberg blocks and front-end components, and supporting deployments and ongoing changes.

The challenge

A corporate site at this level is edited by communications teams, not developers. Content needs to look on-brand every time it is published, without designers checking each page. Custom blocks are how you give editors flexibility and keep the layout under control.

Approach

  1. Blocks as a design system

    Built custom Gutenberg blocks that encode the site's layouts and components, so editors assemble pages from approved pieces.

  2. Front-end implementation

    Implemented the front end of those components.

  3. Support and deploy

    Provided ongoing support and handled deployments.
