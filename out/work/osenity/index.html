Overview

Osenity sells books online in Serbia. I built the store as a fully custom WooCommerce project and owned it from setup to launch, then handed it over for ongoing maintenance. Along the way I mentored a junior developer on the team.

The challenge

Bookstores have very different needs from a typical shop: lots of products with rich metadata, readers who search by more than price, and — in the Balkans — local payment options. Each of those needed custom work.

Approach

  1. Get the catalogue in

    Built a product import add-on so the catalogue can be loaded into WooCommerce instead of being entered by hand.

  2. Help readers find books

    Implemented advanced catalogue filtering on top of WooCommerce.

  3. Pay the local way

    Integrated a payment gateway for Balkan markets.

  4. Grow the team

    Mentored a junior developer during the build.

What I delivered

  • Fully custom WooCommerce bookstore
  • Product import add-on
  • Advanced catalogue filtering
  • Payment gateway for Balkan markets
  • Junior developer mentoring
