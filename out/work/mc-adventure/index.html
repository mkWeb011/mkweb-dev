Overview

MC Adventure runs guided outdoor activities in Malta — climbing, kayaking, abseiling, ziplines and more. Their shop needed to sell experiences, not just products, so I built a fully custom WordPress and WooCommerce theme and a booking system written from scratch.

The challenge

WooCommerce is designed around products in a cart. Experiences behave differently: they are booked rather than shipped. Instead of bending a generic booking plugin to fit, the booking logic was built specifically for how MC Adventure sells.

Approach

  1. A theme built for this brand

    Developed a fully custom WordPress/WooCommerce theme rather than adapting an off-the-shelf one, so layout and checkout follow the business instead of a template.

  2. Booking logic from scratch

    Wrote the booking system from the ground up on top of WooCommerce, so activities are booked through the same store and checkout.

What I delivered

  • Fully custom WordPress and WooCommerce theme
  • Booking system developed from scratch
  • Front-end implementation
