Overview

Zignify helps companies source products globally, handling quality control, logistics and shipping. I own their web stack end to end: domains, DNS, server administration and security, and full-stack WordPress development. On top of that foundation I build the systems that let a small marketing operation publish and rank at a scale that would normally need a much larger team.

The challenge

A sourcing business competes on visibility for a very wide range of product and market searches. Writing, formatting and publishing every article by hand doesn't scale, and hand-built landing pages drift out of date. The site needed to stay technically sound while content and pages were produced continuously — and the marketing side needed CRM and email automation that actually connected to the website.

Approach

  1. Own the foundation

    Took over domains, DNS, server administration and security so that every later automation runs on infrastructure I control and can troubleshoot directly.

  2. Turn tasks into published content

    Built a content engine where a ClickUp task becomes the brief: the system generates an SEO-ready article with AI assistance and publishes it to WordPress automatically, with no copy-paste step in between.

  3. Generate pages from data

    Developed programmatic SEO page systems that create and update large sets of landing pages from structured data, so pages are produced consistently and refreshed when the data changes.

  4. Keep it indexable

    Backed the volume with technical SEO — indexing control, schema markup and Core Web Vitals — so automated output stays crawlable and fast.

  5. Connect marketing

    Set up and connected GoHighLevel (CRM, funnels, workflows) and Klaviyo (email flows, segmentation) to the web stack.

What I delivered

  • Domain, DNS, server administration and security — end to end
  • Full-stack WordPress development
  • ClickUp → AI → WordPress content automation engine
  • Programmatic SEO landing-page systems driven by structured data
  • Technical SEO: indexing, schema, Core Web Vitals
  • GoHighLevel CRM, funnels and workflows
  • Klaviyo email flows and segmentation
